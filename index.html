Everything you glance at in the dark.

Clocks

Eight clock styles, colored per part. Turn the phone sideways and the digits glide onto one line.

Weather & calendar

Animated weather for your location, plus today, month or upcoming meetings from your calendar.

Summary

Up to eight cards on one panel. Hold to rearrange, resize or remove, like the iOS home screen.

Music & Spotify

Controls, search, queue and timed lyrics. Swipe the cover to skip.

Home

Lights, plugs and an HDMI sync box from four brands, laid out like Apple Home.

Timer

A flip or digital countdown with a little runner. Confetti when time is up.

Desktop cards (Mac)

Live Clock, Timer, Music and Home cards that sit on your Mac desktop.

Night & battery

Dim-red night mode with no blue light, OLED burn-in shift, and battery rings.

Built like it matters.

Four problems worth the trouble.

Spotify said yes and played nothing

Spotify returns "OK" even when nothing plays, so Hushdock checks the player and says what went wrong.

Shared rate limits

All installs on one account share a quota, so the app polls less and obeys Spotify's wait time.

A race found by a model checker

A model checker found a bug where unplugging mid-launch left the app open. Fixed, with regression tests.

Battery and smoothness

The clock redraws only when a digit changes, so it stays smooth and sips power.
