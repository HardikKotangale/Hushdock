Turn it, and it adapts

Upright, hours stack over minutes. Turned sideways, they glide back onto one line.

Every digit, its own card

Each digit gets a hinged card, and a digit only flips when it changes.

Night red

Fades to dim red with no blue light, so it never lights up the room.

Weather & calendar

Animated weather for your location, plus today, month or upcoming meetings from your calendar.

Photos

Each picture you choose fades into the next while it drifts slowly. Read where they are: nothing is copied, changed or sent anywhere.

Summary

Up to eight cards on one panel. Hold to rearrange, resize or remove.

Music & Spotify

Controls, search, queue and timed lyrics. Swipe the cover to skip.

Home

Lights, plugs and an HDMI sync box from four brands, with routines along the top and devices below.

Timer

A flip or digital countdown with a little runner. Confetti when time is up.

Desktop cards (Mac)

Live Clock, Timer, Music and Home cards that sit on your Mac desktop.

Night & battery

OLED burn-in shift and battery rings. The clock redraws only when a digit changes, and nothing animates while the window is hidden.

Problem 01

Spotify said yes and played nothing

Spotify returns 204 (accepted) even when nothing plays, so Hushdock checks the player state and says what went wrong.

Problem 03

A race found by a model checker

700 ms launch delay
Plug inUnplugLaunch
BeforeApp opens anyway
AfterLaunch cancelled

A model checker found that unplugging during the 700 ms launch delay left the app open. Fixed, and 6 model-checker traces now replay as regression tests.

Problem 04

Battery and smoothness

10:08
1 of 4 digits redraw

The clock redraws only when a digit changes, so it stays smooth and sips power.
